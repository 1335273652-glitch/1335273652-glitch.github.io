# Visual assets

`portal-hero.jpg` was generated with the built-in image generation tool and compressed to JPEG for the website.

Prompt: Cinematic anime/comic painterly science fiction illustration of a luminous hexagonal dimensional portal tearing through an obsidian futuristic urban alleyway at night. Giant white-violet energy ring in the right half, tiny solitary silhouetted person standing at its base, wet dark stone reflecting lavender light, intricate architectural silhouettes and fine drifting particles. Left 45 percent near black negative space for heading overlay. Rich atmospheric depth, dramatic scale. Restrained palette black, charcoal, lavender #b296f8, white light. Full bleed landscape 16:9; no words, logos, or watermark.

`favicon.svg` and the three feature illustrations are original vector/CSS graphics.
